# Four Word Pass brand assets

The existing cream paper, green ink, literary type, and banknote motif are the brand's starting point. The mark keeps the numeral 4 inside a fictional banknote; a small red forward arrow adds the act of passing it on.

## Deliverables

- `public/logo.svg` and `public/logo.png`: transparent banknote mark, used in the site header.
- `public/wordmark.svg` and `public/wordmark.png`: horizontal name and mark. The SVG uses Georgia with a serif fallback; the PNG is the fixed appearance for external use.
- `public/favicon.svg`, `public/favicon-32.png`, `public/apple-touch-icon.png`, `public/icon-192.png`, `public/icon-512.png`: browser and bookmark icons.
- `public/og-image.jpg`: optimized 1200 × 630 social card, shared across pages. Story pages supply their own title and excerpt.

Palette: paper `#f4efe2`, ink `#17402f`, bill `#e3ead9`, serial accent `#b3261e`. Keep marks on light backgrounds, or use the supplied square icon for a dark container. Leave clear space around the banknote. The assets describe the current sandbox beta.

The logos are SVG assets authored to extend the existing banknote icon. The social artwork was created with the built-in image generation tool, then resized and encoded as a web JPEG. No image-generation API key or fallback CLI was used.

## Final image prompt

Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: final Open Graph social share image for Four Word Pass, a collaborative storytelling web app.
Primary request: Design a beautiful finished wide 1.9048:1 social preview card, 1200 x 630 composition, styled as an elegant vintage banknote meets literary journal. This is a designed graphic, not a website screenshot. Palette: warm cream paper #f4efe2, forest-green ink #17402f, pale sage #e3ead9, small oxblood-red accent #b3261e. Subtle authentic paper grain, crisp engraving lines, sophisticated literary serif typography akin Fraunces, tiny monospaced labels. Generous margins and high contrast, perfectly readable at small thumbnail sizes.
Composition: left roughly 58 percent is editorial typography, right 42 percent holds a diagonally tilted illustrated fictional banknote with intricate guilloche rosette and a large numeral 4, delicate dashed trail suggesting passing it onward. Banknote must be invented, not US currency. No people. No dashboard, no fabricated metrics. A simple small cream/green rectangular banknote logo with a serif numeral 4 appears upper left alongside the brand name.
Text verbatim: upper left "Four Word Pass". Large left headline over three lines "One dollar." then "Many strangers." then "One story." Below in smaller type "Add four words. Pass it on." Bottom left small "fourwordpass.com". Bottom right small oxblood label "SANDBOX BETA". On banknote only numeral "4" and "FOUR WORD PASS".
Keep every line spelled exactly. Avoid adding any other text. Restrained, witty, tactile and polished. Background opaque cream.
